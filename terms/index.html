1. Your account

You need an account to use the member area. Keep your password safe; you are responsible for activity on your account. Usernames must be unique and must not impersonate others.

Each account may be signed in on up to two devices at the same time.

2. Free trial and subscriptions

New members receive a 3-day free trial. After the trial, continued access requires a paid plan.

Monthly, 3-Month and Yearly plans renew automatically at the end of each period until canceled. Lifetime is a one-time payment.

All plans include the same content and features; plans differ only in access period.

3. Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel at any time from Billing. Access continues until the end of the current paid period.

Payments are non-refundable, including renewals charged because a cancellation was forgotten, except where required by law.

4. Community rules

Be kind. Harassment, hate speech, spam, sexual content and sharing personal information are not allowed. See the Community Guidelines.

We may remove content, mute, suspend or permanently ban accounts that break the rules.

5. Younger learners

There is no minimum age. Users under 18 should not use their real name and should get a parent or guardian's consent before using social features.

6. Content

Lessons, audio, images and the Meemi character are owned by Sakura Speak and are licensed for your personal, non-commercial learning only.

7. Changes

We may update these terms. We will notify you of material changes in the app or by email.
