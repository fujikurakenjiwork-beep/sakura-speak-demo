サービス紹介

Everything you need to actually get fluent

Sakura Speak combines a long-term curriculum with game-like motivation and a real community. Here's what's waiting inside.

Let me show you around! ✨

学習コマンドセンター

Student Dashboard

Your home base after login. A playful map of everything you can do, your progress gauge, today's goals, streak, upcoming events and community activity — all in one place.

全80レッスン

Interactive Lessons

Click-through screens with a dedicated narration for each one. Fullscreen mode, a lesson list with green check marks, and lessons that complete when you finish the weekly quiz.

場面別の単語帳

Vocabulary Sheets

170+ sheets organized by real situations — Kitchen, Doctor's Office, Restaurant, Travel, Shopping, Music and more. Study freely in any order.

復習シート

Review Sheets

One-page summaries of every lesson: key phrases, grammar notes, vocabulary and a quick self-check. Perfect for 5-minute reviews.

ミニゲーム

Mini Games

Flashcards and Petal Rush in Easy, Normal and Hard. Rewards are identical on every difficulty, so you can always choose what feels comfortable.

実績とコイン

Achievements & Coins

Bronze, Silver, Gold, Sakura and Platinum achievements for lessons, streaks, community and events. Earn golden coins and spend them on your Meemi.

アバター

Meemi Avatar Studio

Start as the official white, blue-eyed Meemi. Customize fur, eyes, lashes, cheeks and basic outfits for free, then unlock more with coins. Choose from 9 profile picture poses.

チャット

Text Chatrooms

Topic rooms for beginners, study buddies, kanji and culture. Built-in cooldowns, link limits and word filters keep conversations friendly.

ボイスルーム

Voice Rooms

Public, private (code, invite or followers-only), community, scheduled and official event rooms with host / co-host / speaker / listener roles, raise-hand and a speaker queue.

イベント

Events & Calendar

RSVP to speaking hours, study jams and seasonal culture talks. Add any event to Google, Apple or Outlook calendars in one tap.

通知

Reminders

Streak reminders, event reminders and announcements by email and in-app notifications — you choose what you receive.

安全管理

Safety & Moderation

Report, block and mute; temporary suspensions and permanent bans; private profiles; and guardian consent for younger learners before social features.
