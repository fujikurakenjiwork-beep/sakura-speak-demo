よくある質問

Questions?

Who is Sakura Speak for?+

Anyone who wants to genuinely become fluent in Japanese over time — from total beginners (Class A starts with greetings) to learners who want structure, community and motivation.

Do the plans have different content?+

No. Every plan unlocks exactly the same lessons and features. The only difference is how long your access lasts.

How does the free trial work?+

Confirm your email and you get 3 days of full access — no card required. Pick a plan any time to keep going.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes. Subscriptions renew automatically, and you can cancel from Billing at any time. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds aren't provided for forgotten cancellations, so we send reminders.

Is it safe for younger learners?+

There's no minimum age. For users under 18 we recommend not using real names, profiles start private, and we ask for parent/guardian consent before social features.

What devices are supported?+

Desktop, tablet and mobile browsers. Everything is responsive and touch-friendly.
